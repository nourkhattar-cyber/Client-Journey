I1 - Slow response at first contact
  • Observed delays before the first availability answer
  • Client sends "?" or follows up while waiting
  • Warm leads can drop before Stage 1 is completed
I2 - Alternatives not sent fast enough
  • When the maid is unavailable, no shortlist is sent immediately
  • Client is left without a next option
  • Momentum is lost before package stage
I3 - Requirements collected too early or too often
  • Client is asked full requirements before shortlist loops are tried
  • Or requirements are repeated by another team later
  • No continuity between MMR and later stages
I4 - Generic links instead of curated matching
  • Generic website browsing replaces tailored alternatives
  • Inventory shortage is not escalated clearly
  • Client does the search instead of the team
I5 - Fit questions postponed to later stages
  • Cooking, childcare, pets, start date, or contract context checked too late
  • This creates later mismatch and drop-off
  • Stage 1 should create a strong starting match
I6 - Weak routing to next path
  • Peekaboo vs CC Service choice not made clearly
  • No clear end state: continue matching, CC service, or lost lead
  • Client gets stuck between journeys
