A full BPMN-style journey using all approved stages. Each stage is reduced to the essential process, decision, and route so the full flow stays clean, connected, and easy to finish your task with.

Stage 10

Contract & Visa Process

Final End States

Contract Active Client successfully hires the maid.
Replacement Loop Client rejects the maid and returns to matching.
Refund Completed Client exits after the refund is processed.
Dormant Lead Client delays the decision and stops responding.
Lost Lead Client rejects options or the budget never fits.
