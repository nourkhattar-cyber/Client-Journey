Minimal End-to-End Flow

MMR / Peekaboo Client Journey

A simplified, structured view of the full journey from first inquiry to contract, replacement, refund, or drop-off. This version uses your approved stages and keeps only the minimum process logic needed to understand how the flow works.

Starts with Client asks about a maid found on Peekaboo.
Main middle Offer, qualification, budget discussion, interview, payment, and trial.
If successful Contract and visa process begin.
If not successful Replacement loop, refund procedure, dormant lead, or lost lead.
Stage 1

Inquiry & Availability Check

Client asks about a maid. MMR checks availability and only collects full requirements after shortlist attempts fail.

Main Flow

  1. Client asks about a Peekaboo maid.
  2. MMR checks if the maid is available.
  3. If unavailable, send shortlist #1.
  4. If still no selection, send shortlist #2.
  5. If still no selection, collect full requirements.
  6. Send matching profiles.

Key Decisions

  • Maid available?
  • Client selected a profile?
  • Match found?
  • Interested in maids.cc monthly service?

Outputs

  • Proceed to Stage 2
  • Transfer to CC Service flow
  • Continue profile matching
  • Lost lead
↓
Stage 2

Offer & Package Presentation

Once a maid is selected, the commercial offer is sent.

Send

  • Maid profile
  • Salary information
  • 1-week trial package
  • Visa package pricing

Main Risk

  • Package sent before full fit validation.

Next

  • Client reviews the offer.
  • Proceed to Stage 3.
↓
Stage 3

Questions & Qualification

MMR answers detailed questions and validates fit, customer status, and contract context.

Validate

  • References, cooking, childcare, pets, salary, visa ownership, interview, start date
  • Live-in / live-out, nationality, salary expectations
  • Existing contracts, previous contracts, replacement requests, documents

Decision

  • Interested in this maid?

Route

  • YES - Proceed to Stage 4
  • NO - Return to Stage 1 profile matching
↓
Stage 4

Budget & Commercial Discussion

Sales Supervisor handles salary, trial, visa, monthly charges, and budget fit.

Discuss

  • Salary
  • Trial package
  • Visa package
  • Monthly fees
  • Payment expectations

If Budget Does Not Fit

  1. Clarify pricing structure
  2. Offer lower salary profiles
  3. Offer different nationalities
  4. Offer cheaper candidates
  5. Offer maids.cc Monthly Service

Route

  • YES - Proceed to Stage 5
  • CC Service accepted - Transfer to CC flow
  • NO - Lost lead
↓
Stage 5

Interview & Trial Decision

Interview if requested, then ask whether the client wants to proceed with the trial.

If Interview Requested

  1. Schedule Google Meet
  2. Conduct interview
  3. Send thank-you message
  4. Ask: proceed with trial?

If No Interview / Needs Time

  • Ask directly about the trial
  • Send availability reminder
  • Move into follow-up sequence

Route

  • YES - Proceed to Stage 6
  • NO / later - Follow-up #1, Follow-up #2, Dormant Lead
↓
Stage 6

Trial Readiness & Payment

Only proceed when the client is ready to start within 7 days.

If Ready

  1. Collect Emirates ID, IBAN, address, start date, required documents
  2. Send payment link
  3. Receive payment
  4. Create ERP case
  5. Schedule trial and arrival
  6. Share maid contact details

If Not Ready Within 7 Days

  • Send availability reminder
  • Move to follow-up queue

Next

  • Trial officially starts
  • Proceed to Stage 7
↓
Stage 7

Trial Period

The maid arrives, check-ins are performed, and the trial is evaluated.

During Trial

  • Client feedback collected
  • Check-ins performed
  • Performance monitored

Decision

  • Trial successful?

Route

  • YES - Proceed to Stage 10
  • NO - Proceed to Stage 8
↓
Stage 8

Resolution Flow

When the trial fails, the team tries to retain the client before refunding.

Resolution Steps

  1. Understand the reason
  2. Offer replacement
  3. If needed, escalate to supervisor review

Reasons / Decisions

  • Cooking, childcare, personality, refusal to work, mismatch, client changed mind
  • Accept replacement?
  • Still wants refund?

Route

  • Replacement accepted - Return to Stage 1 matching
  • Refund wanted - Proceed to Stage 9
↓
Stage 9

Refund Procedure

Refunds follow a formal internal review and approval path.

Procedure

  1. Case investigation
  2. Internal review
  3. Supervisor approval
  4. Refund approval
  5. Refund processing
  6. Refund confirmation

End State

  • Refund completed

Purpose

  • Formal closure after unsuccessful trial resolution.
↓
Stage 10

Contract & Visa Process

If the trial succeeds, the client moves into contract activation and visa processing.

Process

  1. Contract issued
  2. Contract signed
  3. Remaining payment collected
  4. Visa processing begins
  5. Medical examination
  6. Emirates ID processing
  7. Visa issuance
  8. Maid onboarded

End State

  • Contract active

Meaning

  • The client successfully hires the maid.

Final End States

Contract Active Client successfully hires the maid.
Replacement Loop Client rejects the maid and returns to matching.
Refund Completed Client exits after refund approval and processing.
Dormant Lead Client delays the decision and stops responding.
Lost Lead Client rejects options or the budget never fits.
